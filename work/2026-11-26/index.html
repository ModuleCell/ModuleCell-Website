Olfactory Chord 嗅覺和弦

Humans perceive over a trillion scents through roughly 400 olfactory receptors firing in combinatorial chords. Type any scent, or place and drag receptors across the 20×20 field to compose real-time spectral interference in three organic rhythms—ripple, heartbeat, and breath. 人類僅憑約 400 種嗅覺受體的組合編碼，便能辨識超過一兆種氣味。在 20×20 受體場中輸入氣味、點擊或拖曳節點，以水波、心跳與呼吸三種有機節律，即時譜出高飽和的光譜干涉和弦。
